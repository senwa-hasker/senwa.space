### What is this?

This is an in-depth answering of the 12axes political test (https://12axes.vercel.app/). This answer sheet is what my "ideal opinions" are (from strongly agree to strongly disagree, with neutral in the middle), meaning, perhaps not what I would do in current circumstances, but what I would do in ideal conditions. Sometimes, you're forced to compromise. This isn't that. Some may not need clarification. Enjoy!

### Questions

### 1. I prefer to seek the meaning of my life in religious faith.

Strongly disagree. Religion is not a valid source of any information besides historical accounts to some degree.

### 2. Military service should be compulsory to prepare the country for war.

Strongly disagree. Citizens would go through combat education in the system, yes, but the military itself is optional. The goal of the subject in the system is to give the citizenry appropriate knowledge on self-defense, rebellion, discipline and rationally picking their fights.

### 3. I prefer gradual cultural change to rapid cultural change.

Strongly disagree. "Cultural change" is a near-binary: Either it does not need change, or it needs immediate change.

### 4. I would prefer a society governed by popular assemblies rather than political leaders.

Strongly disagree. A nation should have a group of leaders, granted they have passed all checks and balances (more on this soon), and were elected to the position by educated citizenry.

### 5. Preserving jobs should take priority over minimum-wage increases.

Disagree. Citizens won't need to work to live comfortably and have strong safety nets, and how I decided to apply this question to my system would be "jobs are higher priority than safety nets", which would not even be a thing to consider here, but if I were to pick, the needs of the majority (public sector) take priority.

### 6. The government should limit imports that threaten local industry and jobs.

Strongly agree. The private sector should not be fully free, in both ways of the word: "freedom to" and "freedom from". It is protected by the government from foreign multinational predatory companies, and barred from harming the people or making them dependent on it.

### 7. Adults should be allowed to keep firearms at home for personal protection.

Strongly agree. Guns are at the forefront of social security and political stability on an educated population.

### 8. The gender identity declared by each person must be respected.

Strongly agree. Has no inherent bearing on morality or legality.

### 9. Companies should set the terms of employment with minimal government interference.

Strongly disagree. That's determined by the government, and standardized, as well as possibly helped out if the company is starting out only.

### 10. Aggressive police operations should be used against crime in areas controlled by criminal groups.

Agree. If it were to come down to it, which it would not, criminal groups, i.e. those almost certainly aligned with immorality on a grand scale, require cessation for the people's safety. This is, obviously, presupposed by the government being a well-intentioned, caring, able body.

### 11. Foreign policy should prioritize my country's influence over its neighbors.

Depends. Foreign policy should prioritize the well-being of humanity and the truth. Those would coincide most of the time, but the framing of the question is arguable.

### 12. Sex work should be recognized as legitimate work.

Strongly agree. No inherent bearing on morality.

### 13. My country should pressure other countries when the national interest is at stake.

Depends. The national interest should be the well-being of other countries' people, and if so, strong debate must happen, and military force avoided.

### 14. The state should be allowed to monitor digital conversations to prevent and combat crime.

Strongly disagree. Personal conversations, whether in person or digitally, are protected from the Transparency Act seen on comment sections and information vetting systems on apps tailored for the population.

### 15. Leaders should be chosen by competence, tradition, or succession rather than by popular vote.

Disagree. Competence and knowledge are prerequisites, and voting is what determines a position.

### 16. The central bank should obey the elected government, not act with total independence.

Strongly agree. The government and central bank are one and the same.

### 17. Public services should be provided by private companies when there is competition.

Strongly disagree. Public services are public, and if private companies want to provide a service, it should compete alongside the public sector.

### 18. My country should be able to launch preemptive strikes against enemies preparing to attack.

Strongly disagree. Violence against a foreign nation should not be initiated. Maybe 1% of the time like a humanitarian mission, but even then it is not the country's obligation (i.e. "should" not "ought to")

### 19. My country should deepen trade ties with other countries.

Agree. No inherent bearing on morality, given proper regulation.

### 20. Public education should be managed by local governments rather than the national government.

Strongly disagree. One national curriculum, appraised by all educated.

### 21. The country should reduce its military spending.

Agree. If we need to reduce spending, military is a first to go.

### 22. Immigrants who reject local customs should lose the right to remain in the country.

Strongly agree. "Local customs" here are the truth, recognizing benign vagueness and removing harmful acts and vaguenesses.

### 23. Immigrants should not have to abandon their customs to live well in the country.

Strongly disagree. Same as above.

### 24. People who immigrate to a country should be required to adapt to local customs.

Strongly agree. Same as above.

### 25. Religious traditions should be passed on to future generations.

Strongly disagree. Religion should be removed from public life to avoid harmful acts and vaguenesses, as well as distortion of morality.

### 26. Secret and universal suffrage is non-negotiable, even for poorly informed voters.

Strongly disagree. Voting only works if the people are aware and knowing of the impact and circumstances of their voting and systems.

### 27. Mining in protected forests should be banned, even with low-impact technology.

Strongly agree. Technology must be designed to coexist harmoniously with nature.

### 28. Political decisions should not be based on religious beliefs.

Strongly agree. Religion has no place in morality, law, power, public life or governance.

### 29. The coexistence of different languages and cultures should be encouraged in a country.

Agree. "Different cultures" here mean how one gets ready for sleep and certain rituals that make them feel nice, not child marriage, genital mutilation and other tragedies.

### 30. Regions should have the right to secede if a local majority votes for it.

Agree. If it were to come down to it, the state's fucked.

### 31. I would prefer an authoritarian regime to democracy if it accelerated my country's development.

Strongly disagree. Authoritarian regimes only cause damage down the line and are not worth it.

### 32. In tackling global warming, I prefer prioritizing technological solutions over reducing consumption.

Strongly agree. Technology against climate change should be well-funded, and reducing consumption is the norm anyway.

### 33. I oppose race-based quotas for public-service jobs.

Strongly agree. An educated population is an antiracist one, and social programs would do what's necessary for everyone to live comfortably anyway.

### 34. The word 'God' should not appear in the constitution.

Strongly agree. No religion.

### 35. Public healthcare should follow uniform national guidelines under a central authority.

Strongly agree. One unified strong system with equity of access, stock and regulation.

### 36. My country should be ready to start a war if that secures its vital interests.

Depends. Starting a war is bad and nonsensical, but should it be the only option to secure the well-being of humanity, it may be permissible to start one granted quintillions of factors. I tend more to disagree here.

### 37. When a law conflicts with my religious faith, I should follow my faith.

Strongly disagree. Religion is harmful to morality, law and government.

### 38. Healthcare, education, and water should not operate under the logic of profit.

Strongly agree. Cash comes from the private sector.

### 39. Private companies should take priority in the management of public services.

Strongly disagree. State ownership of essentials is, well, essential, with a private sector full of regulation for certain fields.

### 40. I prefer keeping state-owned companies publicly owned even if they could be sold at market value.

Strongly agree. Same as above, extended.

### 41. My country should support international war-crimes courts, even when allies are judged.

Strongly agree. War crime courts are overwhelmingly good for maintaining order, even if slow.

### 42. I prefer economic sovereignty to low supermarket prices.

Strongly agree. The public sector guarantees proper nutrition, and that should be maintained by its users.

### 43. Regional taxes should follow national rules to prevent tax disputes between regions.

Strongly agree. One national system: Citizens get a form in the mail saying where by pecentage they want their automatically-assigned tax costs.

### 44. Companies considered strategically important should remain under public control.

Strongly agree. The essentials, yadda yadda.

### 45. Technologies that alter the human body, mind, or reproduction should be restricted.

Agree. Cloning should be restricted, though certainly other aspects of technology here are totally fine and go through baseline regulation only.

### 46. Terrorism suspects should be held without trial until the threat has passed.

Strongly disagree. Fair and speedy trials are the backbone of the country, and if they are not given that, the state has failed, and the people are already furious and attempting a fix, by force if necessary.

### 47. I prefer a simple, natural way of life to a life full of technology.

Disagree. Full of good technology is beneficial.

### 48. LGBT couples should be able to show affection in public like any other couple.

Strongly agree. No inherent bearing on morality or legality.

### 49. Governing efficiently matters more than guaranteeing space for the opposition.

Strongly disagree. Efficiency implies transparency and proper dialogue.

### 50. My country should avoid military alliances that could draw it into conflicts abroad.

Agree. Mostly it should avoid them entirely as well as enemies, but the goal is peace (well-being blahblah)

### 51. My country should exercise political leadership over its region.

Strongly disagree. It has its region, that's it. Other countries can look up to it, but not be directly influenced,

### 52. Domestic food production should take priority over cheap imports.

Strongly agree. Means of production should be maintained by its users.

### 53. I support making it easier for foreign investors to invest in my country.

Depends. Mostly no, but if necessary it's not prohibited.

### 54. The government should select and support leading domestic companies in strategic sectors.

Agree. Subsidies and general public sector business good.

### 55. Consensual adult activities, such as drug use, gambling and sex work, should not be criminalized.

Strongly agree. There will be regulation on all these, but people should not be punished; Gambling platforms should be inaccessible, and harmful drugs as well, though their users are victims instead of criminals.

### 56. Municipalities should be able to pass their own laws, even if they differ from national laws.

Strongly disagree. All law is applied everywhere to maximize equality and equal care of all citizens by all citizens.

### 57. A multicultural society should embrace several languages in everyday life.

Disagree. People should be allowed to speak any language freely, but broadcasting, education, law, social services and social cohesion must all depend on one official core language to maximize knowledge access and mutual care.

### 58. I prefer life in big cities to life in the countryside.

Strongly agree. Personal answer, whatever.

### 59. Taxes should be reduced to encourage economic growth.

Strongly disagree. Economic growth is determined by access to services and, if necessary, subsidies.

### 60. My country should not maintain military bases in other countries.

Agree. No expansion without democracy.
