It was unmistakable, the taste. Sophia's fiery desire, doubt, fear, and love. To say Julie's breath was taken away would be an understatement; Nothing was left in her lungs, product of a lifechangingly beautiful movement not even one second long done upon her. And even still, Julie could do nothing but embrace her emotions and the touch of her soulmate of unrivaled beauty. Every small movement of every nerve ending in every part of her body agreed, they were all naturally gravitating toward Sophia's warm comfort. In a moment of carelessness, however, Julie slipped into one of her greatest fears. One moment, possibly able to ruin an entire future. No thought put into it, instinctually, Julie pulls away and breathes in. "I love you". A lull. Julie freezes. Sophia freezes too, but only momentarily. Her response, a slowing down. Her decision, she moved Julie's hair to the side, exposing her neck. Before the moment is over, Sophia's lips are already confidently kissing its general area. "Say that again", she asks, "please".
