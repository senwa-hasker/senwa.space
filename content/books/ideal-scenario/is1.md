When Aubrey first understood they'd be alone for the entire weekend, a lot of things went through their head, like going out to party, copious gluttony or sleeping it all off, but for this stormy evening they decided to just sit and play some games. After all, their new Aero Five, courtesy of their job, had arrived two days prior, and they needed to set things up some more and sit and enjoy a hefty upgrade from their Two. Work can wait, they thought, as playtesting and playing have very distinct atmospheres. Still, they can't help but play any game in a similar mindset now, even if no work is involved. Looking through the library, the poster child really called for them. The Aero Five had one enormous selling point, an exclusive MMO-RPG game, promising to be the most immersive experience to date, with everything being interactable, worlds being dynamic and a perfectly tuned level system, along with magic, tech and creative building, all in one package generously given for free when purchasing the device itself. Even the controller for this generation had two buttons on its back, mainly for interacting in this game, called Wings Online, which other developers could use for some extra gimmick in their games, though they also served as volume buttons outside of Wings. Now seems like the perfect time, so cue up the, and never mind. Somehow, such a complex game opens nearly instantly, and Aubrey now is met with the character creator menu. Turns out, the progress bar is near the bottom of the screen, and it is going quite fast. Nonetheless, the character maker is extremely detailed. Tweaking the colour of each eye, every freckle and beauty spot, even drawing tattoos, all is intuitive and easy. Aubrey goes for an alternative style with the character's makeup, and gives her beautiful blue eyes, light as a sunny winter sky, pale skin and blonde hair, unconsciously giving her a similar look to their partner Miu, feeling her absence in the heart. About half an hour later, with the character ready, Aubrey is asked to give the character a backstory. Considering the game's medieval-style setting, they can give her a distinct class and tell a different life story to what could have happened. Strangely enough, there's an option to just type in a backstory in a text field instead of choices. And type they did, trying to see just how much they could push the engine's limitations.

> [!NOTE] Sophia is a humble worker turned demigod. One day, while working her field, lightning struck her, and from then on she has incredible powers, from flight to super-strength to super-speed. Still, she decides to maintain a lowkey life, simply working errands for her community, as long as they do not watch her, for now. What awaits next is the question.

Aubrey thought, might as well make her powerful to avoid grinding in the early game. With the character done, they are asked to confirm the character, and just before they press continue, the doorbell rings. Strange, they thought, no packages would arrive this late and Miu is quite far away. Maybe something happened? Nonetheless, they put the controller down and half-shout "Be right there".  

One short walk later, they gently unlock and open the door of the apartment, and the sight was unexpected, to say the least. Sobbing, soaked in rainwater, unannounced, holding an overnight bag. It's clear something is very wrong. "Maya? Oh my God, what happened to you?" Aubrey quietly says in a tone of pure care. After some sobs, Maya attempts to regain composure and say something, but is unable to form any words. One thing has changed, however, and that's that her gaze was now deep into Aubrey's eyes, and her expression was nearly that of an abandoned puppy.
